925 Terms & Definitions

Quick reference for weather bands, tools, stats, greens, and course labels used across the hub.

Weather Tools Stats Greens Course Pricing

Weather (avg tournament wind)

Bands are based on average sustained wind for the tournament play window — not peak gusts alone.

Great — very little wind if any (rare). ≤3 mph.
Good — winds ≤ 9 mph (9 mph counts as Good).
Fair — 10–15 mph.
Bad — 16–20 mph.
Worst — 21+ mph.

Tools

925 Cheat Sheet — curated data sheet for that week’s tournament so the heavy lifting is done for you. H1–H4 = most relevant course/event history. S1–S5 = key stats for the week.
Rankings — in golf the lowest number is best. Rank views sort lowest → highest so the best sit at the top.
925 Showdown / Leaderboard — best when updated after each round. Showdown and tournament standing work together. Regression = who is most likely to play better/worse next round. Showdown Rank = best for that round (early tee + good showdown rank is a common lean). Win Rank = most likely to win heading into that round from current position.
925 Lineup Builder — max-enter GPP helper; manual projections or 925 projections.
925 Lineup Tool — quick read on lineup cut chances. Lineups rarely clear ~70% to get 6/6 across the cut; closer to that is better.

Stat terminology

BOB — Birdie or Better %. Share of holes scored birdie or better.
B2B — Birdie to Bogey ratio (birdies+ / bogeys+).
SG TTL / SG Total — strokes better/worse than field avg per round (same course & event context).
SG OT — Strokes Gained Off the Tee (Par 4/5 tee shots vs baseline).
SG AP — Strokes Gained Approach.
SG ARG — Strokes Gained Around the Green.
SG P — Strokes Gained Putting.
SGT2G — Strokes Gained Tee to Green (total minus putting).
GG — Greens Gained (approach to green/fringe vs field).
DD — Driving Distance (avg yards on measured drives).
TD — Total Driving (rank combo of distance + accuracy).
BS — Ball Striking (Total Driving rank + GIR/fringe rank).
TA — Total Accuracy (tee-to-green accuracy measure).
GD% — Good Drive % (fairways + GIR/fringe when miss-fairway on Par 4/5s).
WEDGE — scoring from ≤125 yards vs field.
200+ — scoring from 200+ yards vs field.
Par 4 / Par 5 — scoring average on those hole types.

Greens

In Specialist / Custom Model, Greens filters use Strokes Gained Putting (not total SG). Greens = primary surface. Greens (alt) = secondary / mix.

Bent — popular cool-summer / coastal putting surface; short, even, flat (Pacific NW, Northeast, Midwest).
Bermuda — heat/humidity tolerant; common in the South; fast recovery (e.g. TPC Sawgrass style).
Poa (Poa Annua) — West Coast color; shallow roots; often hand-watered (e.g. Pebble Beach).
Paspalum — tropical / salt-resistant; ball tends to “catch” or grab more.
Rye — common cool-summer turfgrass.

Event scoring

Hard — over par.
Normal — about −2 to even.
Easy — −3 or lower.

Course style

Links — natural coastal landscape; slopes/rolls; wind is often a major factor.
TPC — built for tournaments; open (not tree-lined), not links.
Seaside — by the ocean; typically windy; often links-like.
Parkland — manicured, landscaped, tree-lined green corridors.

Course length

Short — under 7,099 yards
Normal — 7,100–7,399 yards
Long — 7,400+ yards

DFS pricing tiers

High Tier — $12,000–$9,000
Mid Tier — $8,900–$8,000
Low Tier — $7,900–$7,000
Value Tier — $6,900–$6,000
